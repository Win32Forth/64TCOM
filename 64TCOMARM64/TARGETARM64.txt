TARGETARM64.txt — 64TCOM ARM64 pack notes
=========================================
Product:  64TCOM
Pack:     ARM64  (directory 64TCOMARM64)
Host:     64Forth on Apple Silicon (AArch64)
Status:   Named and scaffolded; implementation Phase 3

Load order (planned; mirror GEN)
--------------------------------
  1. ../64TCOMSRC/64HOST.fth
  2. ../64TCOMSRC/64DIR.fth
  3. ASMARM64.fth
  4. OPTARM64.fth
  5. LIBARM64.fth

  Entry: FLOAD TARGETARM64.fth

Endian / cells
--------------
  AArch64 little-endian. Target cell for ITC tokens: likely 8 bytes
  (match host) unless we deliberately choose 4-byte tokens later.

Relationship to classic TCOM
----------------------------
  Classic had many packs (TCOM86, TCOM80, TCOMGEN, …).
  64TCOM follows the same idea:

    64TCOMGEN/     = TCOMGEN (generic)
    64TCOMARM64/   = first production-oriented CPU pack
